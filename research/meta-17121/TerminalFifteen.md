# Unrestricted lower bound of 15 gates on all four projections

All four G11 projections require **at least 15 binary AND/OR gates**, even with unlimited fan-out. The known 19-gate circuits put the unrestricted minimum in the range 15 to 19. This certificate uses the finite truth tables of L-meta-17121 and Python-checked SAT proofs. The host's assignment asks for the stronger lower bound of 19 in Lean.

## Covering every smaller size

Choose a minimum circuit. The [repeated-input argument](ReadOnceInputs.md) excludes sizes through 13 in all four cases and excludes size 14 directly in projection 2. For the other three rows, suppose the size is exactly 14. Normalizing clean AND gates gives d dirty gates followed by t clean products and t−1 ORs, with d=15−2t. Each dirty gate is one on at least one negative sample. A minimum at this size has at least two distinct reused intermediate results, by the [one-shared-gate certificate](OneSharedGate.md). In the normalized circuit only dirty gates can be reused, which leaves t=1 through 6. The ten primary variables all occur, with at most 13 total occurrences. At most three primaries can have two readers. Among all subsets of at most three vertices the cover-closure test leaves one candidate set in projection 0, two in projection 1, and twelve in projection 3.

For each surviving case, `terminal_synthesis.py` encodes the d arbitrary dirty gates and the t final products. Each gate selects two different earlier wires; each product must be false at every negative. The products together cover every positive edge, and each has a private edge since the circuit is minimum. These private edges can order the products without changing the circuit. The first dirty gate is necessarily a raw OR of two repeated inputs. An AND of distinct raw inputs either has no positive edge or never accepts a negative. This permits an optional final split by the pair of raw inputs on the first gate. The positive controls fix all four known 19-gate circuits in precisely the same layout and independently evaluate the extracted gates on the original words.

The supplementary clauses are justified in [the refined synthesis note](RefinedSynthesis.md), [the single-level argument](SingleLevel.md) and [the independent free-operation lower bounds](FreeOperation.md). The free-operation results provide six AND gates and seven OR gates. Excluding no-error-positive-subset and no-error-negative-superset shared signals, canonical evaluation order on the dirty gates, essential raw vertices, and the sole-reader constraints all apply to a minimum circuit after normalization. The implementation allows arbitrary readers of all its dirty signals.

## Replaying the result

CaDiCaL returned UNSAT in all required cases. A separate Glucose instance verified every added clause by reverse unit propagation through a derived empty clause. The complete checks contain 6,819,075 validated additions. A timeout supplies no lower bound; when a larger case exhausted its conflict budget, all first-gate pairs were checked separately. The manifest records individual case keys, verified addition counts, solver statistics and proof-text hashes. The lower part of the argument for projection 2 needs no SAT call at size 14.

Install the versions used by the existing synthesis workflow (Python 3.12 and `python-sat==1.9.dev25`). From this directory run `python terminal_controls.py` and `python verify_terminal14.py`. The latter command regenerates all the proofs and validates their clauses. Passing `--manifest-only` checks coverage of the recorded cases and counts without regenerating proof text. Both forms require Python assertions to be enabled. New solver runs record their own proof bounds if a platform chooses a different sequence of learned clauses.
